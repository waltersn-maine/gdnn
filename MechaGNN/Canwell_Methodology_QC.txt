SCOPE AND DATA PROVENANCE
- 2000 Isabel Pass: Intermap legacy IfSAR DSM; actual acquisition date and vertical datum for the specific Canwell tiles remain unconfirmed.
  Neighboring Isabel tile metadata identifies EGM96, but this is not verified for our three source tiles.
- 2010 Intermap IfSAR: DTM, acquisition June 10-July 1, 2010; vertical NAVD88/GEOID09 (original tile metadata).
- 2025 LiDAR: original source states WGS84 horizontal and vertical
- DoDs cover common valid pixels across all three datasets, INCLUDING non-glacier terrain.
  Consequently, spatial means below are NOT glacier-only elevation change or ice-volume estimates.

2000 DSM-TO-DTM PROCESSING (PROVISIONAL)
- QGIS slope-based DTM filter, raster input 5 m; initial test kernel=3, terrain slope=100, relaxed filter, SD=0.1 was too aggressive.
- Retained candidate: kernel radius=3, terrain slope PARAMETER=500, Relax filter, standard deviation=0.1.
- GDAL Fill NoData: maximum search distance=3 pixels (15 m), smoothing iterations=0.
- Output masked back to the original 2000 DSM footprint using QGIS Raster Calculator.
- Some rejected pixels/gaps remain. Steep natural slopes can be mistaken for non-ground; derived DTM is not an independently validated bare-earth model.
- Preliminary DSM-minus-derived-DTM check found local extremes near -24.45 m and +57.01 m; mean near +0.24 m.

HORIZONTAL GRID PROCESSING
- Original 2000 WGS84 UTM Zone 6N (EPSG:32606) 5 m mosaic used as common reference grid.
- ArcGIS Pro Project Raster: 2010 from NAD83 Alaska Albers (EPSG:3338) to WGS84 UTM Zone 6N (EPSG:32606).
  Bilinear resampling, 5 m cells, snap raster 2000 original DSM mosaic.
  IMPORTANT: Geographic transformation: WGS_1984_(ITRF00)_To_NAD_1983, applied in reverse (NAD83 to WGS84). No vertical datum transformation was performed.
- ArcGIS Pro Resample: original cleaned 2025 1 m LiDAR to 5 m with Bilinear, same snap raster.
- ArcGIS Pro Extract by Mask: 2010 and 2025 clipped using original 2000 DSM survey footprint.
- No formal vertical datum transformation was applied during horizontal processing.

VERTICAL BIAS ADJUSTMENT (PROVISIONAL)
- Four visually road/roadside GCP locations used to estimate the systematic 2025-minus-2010 difference.
- A uniform 15.1903 m was SUBTRACTED from the 2025 DEM over the overlapping survey area.
- This is an empirical calibration from the same four points used in the check, NOT an independent vertical validation.
- Verify long-term stability of road/roadside surfaces and the height datum before scientific interpretation.
